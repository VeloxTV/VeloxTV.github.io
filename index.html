Supercar Shorts,
made with Jarvis.

VeloxTV is a YouTube Shorts channel about supercars - the fastest, loudest and most expensive cars in the world, explained in under a minute.

The app

Jarvis is a private desktop tool that the channel owner uses to make VeloxTV videos. It writes a script from checked facts, records a voiceover, cuts together licensed clips, and - once the owner has watched and approved a video - uploads it to the VeloxTV YouTube channel. It also reads the channel's own view and watch-time numbers so future videos can be improved.

Jarvis runs only on the owner's own computer and only connects to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts, sign-ups or payments.

Jarvis uses YouTube API Services. See the privacy policy for exactly what it accesses and why.
