Privacy
policy.

Last updated 24 September 2026

This policy covers Jarvis, the desktop app used to make and upload videos to the VeloxTV YouTube channel. In short: Jarvis only touches the owner's own YouTube channel, keeps everything on the owner's own computer, and never sells or shares it.

Who runs it

Jarvis and VeloxTV are run by one person, the channel owner, based in Sydney, Australia. Contact: williamsburg14.77alt@gmail.com.

YouTube API Services

Jarvis uses YouTube API Services. By using Jarvis you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What Jarvis accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, Jarvis asks for these permissions, and only for the owner's own account:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload) - to post videos the owner has watched and approved.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly) - to read the channel's name and ID and the list of its own videos, so Jarvis knows which uploaded video is which.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly) - to read the channel's own numbers (views, likes, watch time, how long people keep watching) so future videos can be made better.

Jarvis does not read comments, messages, subscribers' personal details, other people's channels, or anything outside YouTube.

How the data is used

  • To upload approved videos with their title and description.
  • To match uploaded videos to the notes Jarvis kept when it made them.
  • To produce a weekly report for the owner on which kinds of videos performed best.

It is not used for advertising, profiling, or anything else.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on the channel owner's own computer: the Google sign-in token and the saved analytics numbers are plain files in the Jarvis folder. Jarvis has no servers and no cloud database. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Sharing

Data received from Google is never sold, rented, or shared with anyone. The only other services Jarvis talks to are the ones that help make a video (for example AI services that write the script text and read it aloud); they receive the script text only, never YouTube account data.

Limited Use

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long it is kept

The sign-in token is kept until access is removed (see below). Analytics numbers are kept on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them. Removing access or deleting the Jarvis folder deletes everything.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google's security settings at myaccount.google.com/connections (or security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). To have anything deleted, email williamsburg14.77alt@gmail.com.

Children

Jarvis is a private tool used only by the adult channel owner. It is not used by or directed at children and does not collect information from anyone who watches VeloxTV.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
